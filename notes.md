# Notes on Cosmic Expansion

## Hubble's Law

Hubble’s law describes the linear relation between the distance of galaxies and their recession velocity:

v = H₀ · d

This suggests that the universe is expanding uniformly at large scales.

## Physical Meaning

The expansion is not due to galaxies moving through space, but rather space itself expanding.

## Limitations

The linear relation is only an approximation:
- valid for relatively small redshifts
- breaks down at large cosmological distances
- requires General Relativity for accurate modeling

## Connection to Modern Cosmology

At larger scales, expansion is described using:
- the Friedmann equations
- cosmological parameters (Ωₘ, ΩΛ)

## Personal Reflection

What makes this concept fascinating is that a simple linear relation reveals a dynamic universe, where spacetime itself evolves.

This raises deeper questions about the nature of time, space, and the structure of reality.
